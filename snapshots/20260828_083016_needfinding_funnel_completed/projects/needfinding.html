Needfinding in Clinical Environments

Partnering with: Northwestern Medicine

Esophageal cancer, or Esophageal Adenocarcinoma (EAC), is one of the deadliest cancers when caught late, with much higher survival rates when caught early. Northwestern Medicine, in an effort spearheaded by Dr. Sri Komanduri, partnered with Northwestern's EDI and Biomedical graduate programs to bring a fresh lens to identifying the patients most at risk of developing it.

The focus of the class was on “increasing the denominator,” which meant finding high-risk patients who currently go unidentified. As an entirely new class at Northwestern, we spent the quarter identifying and understanding what problems existed, how they should be classified, and what stakeholders to hand them off to.
