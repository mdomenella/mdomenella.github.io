Role

New Product Development Engineer

Jul 2023 - Jun 2025

2 Years

As a New Product Development Engineer at Clippard, I led 3 products through release and contributed to 2 more in internal development. All five were built for the customers in the biomedical industry, in use cases such as infant ventilator, a full-scale ventilator, at-home kidney dialysis systems, and cell and gene therapy equipment.

Below I have highlighted two of my released products - the NPV3 Panel Mounted Pinch Valve and the NPV7 Panel mounted pinch valve.

01. NPV3 PANEL MOUNTED PINCH VALVE

Solving for User Friction in Pinch Valves

After identifying that a significant number of customers were creating custom solutions to mount their valves to a panel, I was given the responsibility of designing what a one-size-fits-all, panel-mount-ready pinch valve should look like.

Rather than designing solely around the installation process for the company purchasing the valve, we looked past the point of sale to the people who would actually live with the product day to day. This led me to three key touchpoints across the valve's life that were critical to a good design: installation, maintenance, and use.

02. NPV7 BETA UNIT FAILURE ANALYSIS

Client Collaboration for Optimal Performance

When failures emerged during beta unit testing for a custom valve in an at-home hemodialysis machine, I stepped in to work directly with the client's engineering team to get to the bottom of it.

Solving the issue meant staying in biweekly communication with the client while I dug into the problem from every angle, replicating their exact testing conditions in our own lab to rule out variables one by one. That rigorous approach uncovered the root cause and led to a fix that eliminated the client's beta failure rate.
