Clippard Inc.

Cell & Gene Therapy Valves

As a Mechanical Engineer at Clippard, I designed panel-mounted pinch valves for cell and gene therapy applications, where precise, contamination-free fluid control is critical to the manufacturing process.

The work spanned concept through production: defining requirements with the customer, developing CAD models, running prototypes, and validating performance against tight reliability and cleanliness specifications.

The result is a compact, serviceable valve assembly that integrates cleanly into existing equipment panels while meeting the demands of advanced medical manufacturing.
