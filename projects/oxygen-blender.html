Clippard Inc.

Oxygen Blender

I contributed to the development of an oxygen blender for a baby open care warmer, a device that mixes air and oxygen to a precise concentration for newborns in intensive care.

This project demanded a careful balance between pneumatic performance and the reliability required of neonatal medical equipment. I worked on component selection, flow path design, and verification testing to ensure consistent, accurate blending.

Designing for such a sensitive patient population reinforced how much rigorous testing and thoughtful detailing matter when lives depend on the product.
